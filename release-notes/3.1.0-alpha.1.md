Nuvio Z Installer puts Nuvio Z on your iPhone using your own Apple Account. Choose the download for your Windows PC, Mac, or Android phone. After installation, Nuvio Z can renew itself on the iPhone.

## What's new

- **Install from Android:** connect your iPhone by USB and follow the same guided setup available on desktop. Requires an arm64 Android phone with USB host support.
- **The installer can update itself:** it checks for a newer version when opened. Windows and Mac download, verify, replace the installer and restart; Android asks you to approve the APK update.
- **Important signing updates are highlighted:** when an update changes compatibility with Apple's signing service, the installer recommends updating before starting.
- **Repair an existing setup:** Android's "Add it again" restores Nuvio Z's connection file without removing the iPhone app.
- **One download home:** Windows, universal Mac and signed Android packages are now released together in the dedicated Nuvio Z Installer repository.

## Downloads

- **Windows x64:** unzip `Nuvio-Z-Installer-Windows-x64.zip` and open the installer.
- **Mac:** unzip `Nuvio-Z-Installer-macOS.zip`, move the app to Applications, and open it. Supports Intel and Apple Silicon on macOS 11 or later. The app is not notarized; if macOS blocks it, use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. Instructions are included in the zip.
- **Android:** open `Nuvio-Z-Installer-Android.apk` and allow installation from your browser or files app when Android asks. Install updates over the existing app to keep your pairing data.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for all three downloads.

## Alpha testing

All three release packages passed CI builds and automated checks. Installing Nuvio Z from Android has passed a real phone-to-iPhone test on an earlier proof build. The new installer's self-update replacement and restart flows still need a real upgrade test.
